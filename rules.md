## 🃏 Game Components & Setup

### Components

* **Standard Deck of Cards** (52 cards, Jokers removed)
* **Money Cards (2–10):** Set the baseline pip movement (1 point = 100 pips).
* **Event Cards (Jack, Queen, King, Ace):** Apply economic/geopolitical multipliers to the Money Card result.

* **Player Capital:** Each player starts with **$100 USD** in cash.

### Core Parameters & Leverage

* **Base Margin:** **25x** maximum leverage.
* **Capital Limit:** A player with **$100** can place positions up to **$2,500** total exposure.
* **Bet Increments:** All bets must be made in **$25 units** of total position size ($25, $50, $75, ..., $2,500).
* *Margin Required:* A $25 bet requires **$1** of your cash collateral; a $2,500 bet requires **$100** of collateral.

## 💰 The 3-Pile Capital Management System

At the start of every round, each player takes their total cash bankroll and divides it into three distinct piles:

1. **The Bet (Investment Amount):** The principal capital allocated to the trade position. This determines your position size when multiplied by your **25x leverage**.
2. **The Collateral (Buffer):** Capital set aside strictly as margin protection against negative pip movements. This acts as your shock absorber.
3. **The Uninvested Reserve (Cash):** Safe, liquid cash kept on the sidelines that cannot be touched by market losses during this round. Available for future rounds or to adjust strategy.

---

## 🚨 The Liquidation & Forfeiture Rule

Because trades are leveraged at **25x**, small market moves translate into large pip gains or losses.

* **Safe Settlement:** If a trade loses money, but the loss is **less than or equal to** the Collateral pile, the loss is deducted from the Collateral, and the Bet is returned with whatever is left.
* **Liquidation (The Wipeout):** If the market moves against a player so violently that their losses **exceed their Collateral pile**, the position is liquidated: **the entire Bet amount AND the entire Collateral pile are instantly forfeited to the bank.**
* *(Note: The Uninvested Reserve pile is safely untouched, preventing a player from going into permanent debt, but wiping out their active trading capital).*

2. **2. Position & Bet Phase:** Choose Long/Short based on 25x leverage.
Players commit their Bet amount (multiplied by 25x for total exposure) and declare their direction: **Long USD (Red)** or **Long JPY (Black)**.


3. **3. Market Catalyst & Draw:** Draw Event Card (+1 to +4) and Money Card (2-10).

* **Winners:** Collect profit proportional to their leveraged Bet size.
* **Losers (Within Buffer):** Loss is subtracted from Collateral.
* **Losers (Exceeds Buffer):** Total forfeiture of Bet + Collateral to the bank.

## 🔄 Updated Settlement & Position-Holding Mechanics

At the end of Step 4 (Daily Market Settlement), if a player's trade is in a **loss**, they choose one of two paths based on their available collateral:

```
                  ┌────────────────────────┐
                  │ Market Move is a Loss  │
                  └───────────┬────────────┘
                              │
               Is Loss > Collateral Buffer?
               ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
              YES                            NO
               │                             │
               ▼                             ▼
       🚨 LIQUIDATION              Player Chooses Action:
   • Forfeit entire Bet        ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
   • Forfeit Collateral        │                           │
   • Position is Closed     OPTION A:                   OPTION B:
                          Settle Loss                 Hold Position
                          • Deduct loss from          • Write down current
                            Collateral                  unrealized loss
                          • Close position            • Roll position into
                                                        the next round!

```

---

### 🟢 Option A: Settle the Loss (Close Position)

* The loss amount is deducted from the **Collateral pile**.
* The **Bet pile** and remaining **Collateral** are returned to the player's cash pool.
* The position is closed.

### 🔴 Option B: Hold & Roll Over (Carry Position Forward)

* **Condition:** Only available if the current loss is **less than or equal to** the Collateral pile.
* **Mechanism:** The player writes down their **Unrealized Loss** (e.g., $-10\text{ points / } -1,000\text{ pips}$) on their score tracking sheet.
* **Next Round:** The player leaves their **Bet** and **Collateral** locked on the board for the next day:
* If the next day's market moves **in their favor**, it reduces (or erases) their written-down loss and adds to their profit.
* If the next day's market moves **further against them**, the new loss adds to the existing loss. If the *combined cumulative loss* now exceeds the Collateral buffer $\rightarrow$ **Instant Liquidation!**

## 🔄 The Roll Over & Carrying Fee Rule

If a trade results in a **loss** at the end of daily settlement, and that loss is **less than or equal to the player's Collateral buffer**, the player can choose to carry the position into the next round instead of closing it.

### The Cost to Roll Over:

1. **Pay the $5 Fee:** The player must pay **$5 in cash** directly to the bank from their Uninvested Reserve (or cash pool) as an overnight carrying fee.
2. **Lock the Bet & Collateral:** The original Bet and Collateral piles remain locked on the board for the next round.
3. **Record the Unrealized Loss:** The player writes down their current loss (e.g., $-15$) on their score tracker. This loss carries forward and will either be offset by tomorrow's market movement or added to tomorrow's result.

---

## 📋 Summary of Settlement Choices for Losses

When a player faces a losing trade within their collateral limit, they choose one of two paths:

| Choice | Action Required | Financial Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Option A: Settle & Close** | Take the loss today. Deduct the loss amount from the Collateral pile and return the rest. | Loss is locked in. Cash is freed up. **No $5 fee.** |
| **Option B: Roll Over & Hold** | Keep position open for tomorrow. Record the unrealized loss. | Pay **$5 cash fee** to the bank. Position stays active for another round. |

## 3. Best Practices for Trading with an Economic Calendar

1. **Check Local Time Zone Sync:** Always ensure the calendar's clock matches your local time zone so you don't get caught off-guard during market opens.
2. **Watch the "Deviation" (Actual vs. Forecast):** Currency markets rarely react to the number itself; they react to how much the **Actual** figure deviates from the market **Consensus/Forecast**.
3. **Be Aware of Volatility Spikes:** Spread widening and slippage often occur seconds before and after major releases (like US CPI or FOMC decisions). Conservative traders often wait 5–15 minutes post-release for price direction to stabilize.
